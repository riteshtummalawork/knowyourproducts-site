Precision in assessment

Every answer. Every effort. Every opportunity.

Protryx is an online platform for quizzes, exams and coding labs. The server keeps time and marks answers, the browser reports integrity signals, and staff review each flagged event before results are released.

Features

What Protryx does

For candidates

A focused exam screen with server-side timing, so the clock is the same for everyone, and question order set per candidate.

For staff

Build exams and coding labs, grade answers, and review integrity events with the evidence behind each one.

Integrity, reviewed by people

Protryx records and flags events such as tab switches for a person to review. It does not decide on its own that someone cheated.

Accounts and records

Two-step verification for staff accounts and a full audit log of who did what, and when.
